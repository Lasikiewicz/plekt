Select a movie, show, episode, provider result, or person to open its detail context.

The detail page brings identity, artwork, watch state, related content, and destination
actions together so a correction can be made against the correct record.


## Page actions

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-detail-overview-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-detail-overview-light.png"
  alt="Plekt movie detail header with artwork, watch status, facts, ratings, and playback links"
  caption="The header brings title identity, canonical watch state, ratings, and playback links together."
/>

The top action bar keeps personal actions visible. Open **Options** for watch-state,
sync, artwork, matching, and information controls for the current title. TV pages also
show **Hide Spoilers** beside the show-level actions:

- **Hide Spoilers** hides the synopsis and artwork for unwatched episodes while you inspect
  a show. It changes presentation only.
- **Force Sync** opens a title-scoped sync operation. Use it when one title needs its local
  watched and resume state replayed without starting a whole-library operation.
- **Mark Unwatched** clears the current canonical watched state after confirmation and
  sends the correction through the normal sync pipeline.
- **Edit Images** opens the artwork picker described in [Edit Images](#edit-images).
- **Fix Match** opens the provider identity search described in [Fix Match](#fix-match).
- **Merge** combines the current TV show's episode records with another show. Make a backup
  and check the preview before confirming because this changes the local record structure.
- **Delete** removes the tracked title after confirmation. It is not a way to remove a file
  from Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin; back up first and use it only when the local record itself
  should be removed.
- **Info** opens a read-only view of watch totals, the latest watched date, sync results,
  season and episode watch records, and the available audit detail. **Export** saves the
  information for later investigation.

The bar is deliberately title-scoped.

Library-wide repairs and imports live under [Settings → Sync](/docs/settings/#sync), while
card-level quick actions live in the [poster three-dot menu](/docs/libraries/#the-poster-three-dot-menu).

## What the page can contain

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-sections-overview-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-sections-overview-light.png"
  alt="Plekt movie detail page showing the header, Cast, and Images sections"
  caption="The movie detail page places provider content below the title header; Appearance controls which sections are shown."
/>

Depending on the media type and configured providers, details can include:

- poster, backdrop, logo, overview, and provider identifiers;
- a **Media facts** table for status (with the release date in the status row), genres,
  network or studio, language, and runtime;
- TMDB, TheTVDB, and optional IMDb ratings as logo badges with their scores;
- TV seasons, episodes, air dates, and watched progress;
- cast members and person pages;
- trailers and clips;
- reviews, image galleries, recommendations, and related shows;
- network and availability information;
- Seerr request controls when Seerr is connected; and
- links back to a matching Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin item.

Optional sections are controlled by [Appearance](/docs/appearance/#media-detail-options).
For page-specific guidance, see [Movies media page](/docs/movies/media-page/#appearance)
or [TV Shows media page](/docs/tv-shows/media-page/#appearance).

Provider metadata describes the title, but it does not prove that the title exists in every
connected library.

Use the local match and availability information for that decision.

## TV detail controls

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-spoilers-off-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-spoilers-off-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Bridgerton detail page with Season 3 expanded and Hide Spoilers unchecked"
  caption="With Hide Spoilers disabled, Bridgerton Season 3 shows its episode artwork, synopses, watched state, and actions."
/>

TV show details add season and episode controls below the identity and progress summary:

- Season rows load their episode details when expanded, so the initial show page can render
  without fetching every season before the header and summary appear. **Expand All** opens
  every season progressively; the season rows can also be opened individually.
- Season navigation, artwork, and episode loading stay incremental. Reserved image space and
  shimmer placeholders keep the page stable while off-screen artwork loads, and a refresh keeps
  the mounted detail view visible instead of replacing it with a blank page.
- Each season row reports its episode total, watched count, and known availability. A season
  with no availability is still a valid metadata record; it simply has no matching copy on
  the connected media servers currently reporting to Plekt.
- Episode rows expose the watch, unwatch, date, and title-scoped sync actions appropriate to
  that episode.
- Episode rows and history cards prefer the real stored episode name. If an episode name is
  missing, run [Settings → Tools → Database Repairs → Restore Missing Episode Names](/docs/settings/tools/#database-repairs).
  A verified metadata record with no title is shown as **No title provided**.
- **Request 4K** appears when Seerr is connected and the configured request service exposes
  a 4K request path. It creates a request; it does not mark the episode watched.

Bulk show and season actions preserve episode identity and do not treat unaired episodes as
completed watches. **Mark season watched** changes only episodes that are still unwatched and
eligible; existing watched episodes remain unchanged. **Mark the show watched** applies the
same rule across every eligible released season, with an opt-in choice for specials. The date
dialog can use each episode's release date, so a whole-series action preserves release-day
watch dates instead of assigning the current day to every episode. When every episode in the
selected scope is already watched, the action becomes a resync, which replays state without
creating another watch event. Episode rows show their saving state and progress as the live
watch events arrive, while the action remains open until the final destination result settles.

If the count is wrong, fix the show identity before applying a bulk action.

## Watch actions and dates

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-watch-status-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-watch-status-light.png"
  alt="Plekt movie detail Watch Status section showing the watched date and completion percentage"
  caption="The detail page keeps the canonical watched date and completion percentage alongside the movie identity."
/>

### Mark watched

On a movie or episode, **Mark Watched** opens the date choices supplied by the app.

These can include today, the release date, a date relative to a nearby episode, or a custom
date.

The saved date becomes part of the canonical watch record and is sent through the normal
sync pipeline.

On a TV show, a watch action can target one episode, one season, or the whole show.

Bulk actions preserve each episode's identity and do not count unaired episodes as
completed. A watch or unwatch on one show never changes the same episode of a different show
that shares its title.

If everything in the selected scope is already watched, the action becomes a resync.

The existing state can then be sent again without creating another watch event.

### Mark unwatched

**Mark Unwatched** clears the current canonical watched state.

It is a deliberate correction and is propagated to connected destinations.

It does not delete the title's provider metadata.

### Edit watch date

Use **Edit watch date** when the watch happened but the recorded date is incorrect:

1. Open the title detail page or the poster three-dot menu.
2. Choose **Edit watch date**.
3. Select an existing viewing, or choose **Add watch date** for a genuine rewatch.
4. Set the date and time.
5. Save and wait for the action to finish.
6. Check the destination result in [Sync Activity](/docs/sync-tools/).

Removing a date requires confirmation.

If other dates remain, the newest remaining watch is the canonical watched date.

If none remain, the title becomes unwatched.

## Fix Match

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/fix-match-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/fix-match-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Fix Match dialog showing a search across all sources, result cards with their source, and YouTube fallback"
  caption="Fix Match searches your library, TMDB, and (for TV) TheTVDB at once, and also provides a YouTube fallback for unmatched video content."
/>

**Fix Match** changes the provider identity of the local record.

Use it when a title is attached to the wrong movie or TV series.

It is different from **Edit Images**.

An artwork search changes the images being browsed, while Fix Match changes the identity
used by the archive and future syncs.

### Fix a movie match

1. Open the movie detail page, or open the movie card's three-dot menu.
2. Choose **Fix Match**.
3. Search by title, year, or a distinctive part of the title. **Search all sources** checks
   your library and TMDB together.
4. Compare the result's year and metadata with the local movie.
5. Select the correct result.
6. Confirm the change.
7. Reopen the detail page and check its provider details, poster, and Sync Activity.

The selected TMDB identity is saved to the local watch record.

Existing history is reconciled with the corrected identity rather than being left
permanently under the old one.

### Fix a TV show match

1. Open the show detail page. For an episode card, use the show-level match action so the
   series identity is corrected for the whole show.
2. Choose **Fix Match**.
3. Search for the series and compare title, year, network, and season information.
4. Select the correct series. Each result card names the sources it came from; the show is
   rematched by its TheTVDB series.
5. Confirm the change.
6. Wait for the background metadata and progress refresh.
7. Confirm the intended seasons and episodes, then check Sync Activity before retrying a
   skipped destination.

TV Fix Match applies the selected series identity across its episode records, then refreshes the
corrected TV metadata and warms the relevant posters before returning to the show page. Check
Sync Activity for any destination retry that still needs attention. For Trakt `not_found` results,
use the show-wide [Fix show match and retry](/docs/sync-tools/#trakt-show-match-repair) action.

Episode links and notifications use the explicit parent-show identity when one is available,
so an episode provider ID cannot accidentally open a different series route. If the parent
identity is missing, the app keeps the episode coordinates on a title/search route until the
match is repaired.

If no reliable identity exists, use the
[Sync Issues match report](/docs/sync-tools/#sync-issues-and-match-report).

## Edit Images

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-edit-images-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-edit-images-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt artwork picker showing Poster, Logo, Background, and YouTube Show tabs with provider artwork choices"
  caption="Edit Images separates artwork types and lets you choose a provider result or upload a custom image."
/>

**Edit Images** changes the artwork displayed for the local record.

The picker can browse poster, logo, and backdrop images from TMDB, TheTVDB, and Fanart.tv
when those providers are configured.

1. Open the title detail page.
2. Choose **Edit Images**.
3. Select the artwork type to change.
4. If the provider identity is wrong, use the picker search to browse the correct title.
5. Select an image and save.
6. Reopen the detail page if the old artwork is still cached.

Selecting a title inside the artwork picker changes the title used to browse images.

It does not relink the watch record.

Use Fix Match for that.

## Personal lists and the Tools menu

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-light.png"
  alt="Plekt media detail page showing watchlist, custom-list, rating, and watch-state actions"
  caption="Personal actions stay visible in the detail header and do not change canonical watch state."
/>

### Personal actions

Use **Add to watch list**, **Add to playlist**, and the personal rating control to change
local choices from the detail page. Watchlist and playlist actions apply to movies and
TV shows; episode detail keeps the personal rating action. The labels reflect current state: a saved title offers
**Remove from watch list**, a playlist member offers **In playlist**, and a scored title shows its
current 1–10 value.

Watchlist, ratings, and playlists never mark an item watched or change the provider identity
used by sync. A playlist can also sync to Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin. See the dedicated
[Watchlist](/docs/watchlist/), [Ratings](/docs/ratings/), and
[Playlists](/docs/playlists/) guides.

### Tools menu

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-tools-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-tools-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt media detail Tools menu showing Force Sync, Info, Edit Images, Fix Match, and Delete"
  caption="The shared Tools menu is a persistent title-scoped entry point for maintenance and evidence, separate from personal actions."
/>

The permanent **Options** menu is present on movie and TV detail pages. Open it when the
current title needs maintenance rather than a Watchlist, Ratings, or Playlists change.

#### Force Sync

Choose **Force Sync** when one title needs its local watched and resume state replayed or
when you need to pull that title's state from a selected destination. It is title-scoped and
does not replace the broader tools under [Settings → Sync](/docs/settings/sync/).

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/settings-sync-tools-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/settings-sync-tools-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Sync Tools settings showing repair, push, and pull controls"
  caption="Sync Tools shows the wider repair and source-of-truth directions beside the title-level Force Sync menu."
/>

For a single title:

1. Open **Tools → Force Sync**.
2. Choose the direction and destination shown by the dialog. Push sends Plekt's recorded
   watched or resume state out; pull imports the selected title's state from a connected server.
3. Review the preview before confirming, especially when pushing because remote state can be
   overwritten.
4. Check the result in [Sync Activity](/docs/sync-tools/).

For wider scope, use **Repair Recent Items**, **Set Plekt as Source of Truth**, or
**Import Watched Status** under [Settings → Sync → Sync tools](/docs/settings/sync/#sync-tools).

#### Info

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-info-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-info-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Info panel showing watch status, latest watched date, sync results, and audit details"
  caption="Info is read-only evidence for the selected title, including per-destination results and audit details."
/>

Choose **Info** to inspect the latest watched date, the current watch status, destination
results, and available audit events. Use **Export** when you need to preserve the panel for
an investigation. Info does not change the title.

#### Edit Images from Tools

Choose **Edit Images** when the provider identity is correct but the poster, logo, or
background is wrong. Select an artwork type, choose a provider result or custom image, and
save. The full workflow is documented in [Edit Images](#edit-images).

#### Fix Match from Tools

Choose **Fix Match** when this record belongs to the wrong movie or TV series. Compare the
candidate identity before confirming; the full movie and TV workflows are in
[Fix Match](#fix-match).

#### Delete

Choose **Delete** only when the local tracked title and its recorded history should be
removed. It does not delete files from Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin. Review the confirmation scope
and keep a verified backup first; for a wrong date or match, use the targeted corrections
instead.

#### Merge

On a TV show, **Merge** folds one show's episode records into another. Compare the series
identity and preview, make a backup, and confirm only when the records are truly duplicates.
Merge changes local record structure and is harder to reverse than a metadata refresh.

Movie and TV pages expose the actions appropriate to their media type. Merge is TV-only;
Delete is available on both.

Create a backup first and read [Backups and operations](/docs/operations/).

## Related content and lightboxes

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-collections-recommendations-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-collections-recommendations-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt media detail page showing a complete collection row and recommended movie row"
  caption="Collections and recommendations stay in complete horizontal rails; related titles open their own detail context."
/>

- Select the main poster or an image tile to open the image lightbox.
- Trailer and clip tiles play in the media lightbox.
- Cast members open person pages with biographies and filmographies.
- Recommendations and related titles open their own detail context.
- Provider links open the external service for the selected identity.

The website documentation images can be clicked to zoom and dragged while zoomed.

The app's detail-page lightbox follows the app's own controls and media-provider behavior.
