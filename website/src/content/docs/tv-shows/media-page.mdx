The TV Shows media page is the detail page for one selected series. This guide is about
the page reached from a show card, not the TV Shows library's search, filters, sorting, or
view controls.

For example, a direct TV show detail route looks like:

`/tvshow/tmdb/108978-reacher#season4`


## Open a TV show media page

Select a show card in [TV Shows](/docs/tv-shows/) to open its media page. The page keeps
the series context, season list, episode progress, actions, and provider content together.

<ScreenshotFigure

/>

## Show header and facts


The header can include:

- the poster, backdrop, logo, show title, and synopsis;
- a **Media facts** table with **Status**, which reads as release date then show status (for
  example `Released - 14 Aug 2020 - Continuing`), **Genres**, **Network**, **Language**, and
  **Runtime**;
- **Available on**, **Watch Now**, and **Ratings** groups, with external ratings shown as
  logo badges with their scores;
- sync status and media-server availability;
- provider identifiers and links back to Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin; and
- a Seerr request control when Seerr is connected.

Provider data is shown when it is available. A missing provider result does not remove the
local series or episode records.

## Seasons and episodes

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-seasons-episodes-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-seasons-episodes-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Lioness Season 3 accordion showing episode cards, watched state, release dates, and episode actions"
  caption="Each expanded season keeps its episode artwork, watched state, release date, and actions together."
/>

Opening a show without a season in the link expands the latest regular season and keeps
the page at the top. A link to Season 1 also starts at the top; links to other seasons
smoothly scroll to the named season after its details load.

On desktop, season cards show their posters in a column beside the expanded season's
episode cards. On narrower screens, episodes follow their season card. Each episode keeps
its artwork, watched state, release date, and actions together.

The **Seasons** section lists each season with its episode count, watched count,
availability, and next-airing context. Episode details load as a season is expanded, so a
large show can render its header and season summary without waiting for every episode.

Season navigation and artwork load incrementally. The page reserves image space and uses a
shimmer while artwork arrives, so the show header and controls remain usable on a cold load.

- Select a season row to expand or collapse its episodes.
- Use **Expand All** or **Collapse All** to change the season accordion state.
- Episode rows show episode identity, air date, watched state, and available actions once
  that season has loaded.

Episode rows prefer the real episode name stored on the watch record. Verified metadata with no
title remains **No title provided**.

> If the actual episode name is missing, run [Settings → Tools → Database Repairs → Restore
> Missing Episode Names](/docs/settings/tools/#database-repairs). The repair fills the name
> when provider metadata supplies one.

The page can also preserve a season or episode in the URL when opening a direct detail
route, so a shared link can return to the relevant part of the show.

Episode cards and notifications resolve through the explicit parent-show identity. An episode
provider ID is not substituted for the series ID in a `/tvshow/...` route, which keeps deep
links on the correct show.

## Progress and show actions

The header summarizes watched episodes against the known episode total. The action row
contains the controls appropriate to the series state.

### Personal list actions

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-light.png"
  alt="Plekt TV show detail page showing the watchlist and playlist actions"
  caption="Save a show for later or add it to a playlist without changing episode history."
/>

Use **Add to watch list** or **Add to playlist** for local personal choices. Their labels
change to **Remove from watch list** and **In playlist** when the show is already saved.
Read the dedicated [Watchlist](/docs/watchlist/) and [Playlists](/docs/playlists/)
guides for those workflows. Episode ratings are covered in [Ratings](/docs/ratings/#rate-from-a-detail-page).

### Watch-state actions

Use **Mark Watched** or **Mark Unwatched** for the show's episode set. The show-level action
can mark every eligible released episode across all seasons; the date dialog can apply each
episode's release date, and specials can be included explicitly. A season-level **Mark season
watched** action updates only episodes that are still unwatched and eligible; watched episodes
remain unchanged. As the action runs, episode rows and season summaries show **Saving** and
live completion progress without replacing the detail page. When the selected scope is already
fully watched, the control becomes **Resync** and replays the existing state without creating a
new watch event. Use [Sync Activity](/docs/sync-tools/) to confirm destination results.

### Tools

<ScreenshotFigure
  compact={true}
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-tools-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-tools-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt media detail Tools menu showing the common Force Sync, Info, Edit Images, Fix Match, and Delete actions"
  caption="The shared Tools menu is always available for title-scoped maintenance; TV shows also add Merge."
/>

Use the title-scoped **Options** menu for maintenance, sync evidence, artwork, identity
corrections, and TV record management. The sections below explain each action and when to
use it.

#### Force Sync

Choose **Force Sync** when one show needs a targeted watched-state or resume-state replay.
The menu is title-scoped, so it does not replace the broader tools in [Settings →
Sync](/docs/settings/sync/).

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/settings-sync-tools-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/settings-sync-tools-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Sync Tools settings showing repair, push, and pull controls"
  caption="Settings → Sync provides broader push and pull choices when a title-level Force Sync is not enough."
/>

For a single show or episode:

1. Open **Tools → Force Sync** on the detail page.
2. Select the direction and destination shown by the dialog. Push sends Plekt's recorded
   watched or resume state out; pull imports the selected title's state from a connected
   server.
3. Review the preview before confirming, especially when pushing because remote state can be
   overwritten.
4. Check the result in [Sync Activity](/docs/sync-tools/).

For a whole-show push to all destinations, local media-server writes finish before the Trakt
history phase. Trakt writes are paced and retried after rate limits; cancelling stops queued
episodes after the current remove/add pair completes, so a replay is not left half-applied.

Use **Repair Recent Items**, **Set Plekt as Source of Truth**, or **Import Watched Status**
under [Settings → Sync → Sync tools](/docs/settings/sync/#sync-tools) for wider repair and
source-of-truth operations. The watched threshold and resume minimum are controlled in
[Sync Tuning](/docs/settings/sync/#sync-tuning).

#### Info

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-media-info-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-media-info-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt The War Between the Land and the Sea Info panel showing watched episodes, destination results, and audit events"
  caption="Info is read-only evidence for the selected show's watched-episode totals, destination results, and audit-event count."
/>

Choose **Info** to inspect the show's watch status, latest watched record, destination
results, and available audit events. Use **Export** when you need to preserve the evidence.
Info does not change seasons, episodes, personal lists, or the provider match.

#### Edit Images

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-edit-images-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-edit-images-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt TV show Edit Images picker showing artwork choices"
  caption="Edit Images changes the selected show's poster, logo, or background without changing its identity."
/>

Choose **Edit Images** when the series match is correct but its artwork is not. Pick the
artwork type, choose a provider result or custom image, and save. Use **Fix Match** when
the series itself is wrong.

#### Fix Match

Choose **Fix Match** when the show is attached to the wrong series. Search for the series,
then compare each result's title, poster, overview, source labels, and provider IDs before
confirming. TMDB results are resolved to their TheTVDB series identity. A TV match applies
to the series and its episode records.

#### Merge

Choose **Merge** only when two local series records represent the same show. Compare the
series identity, episode totals, and preview, make a backup, and confirm only when the
records are true duplicates. Merge changes local record structure and is harder to reverse
than a metadata refresh.

#### Delete

Choose **Delete** only when the local tracked show and its recorded history should be
removed. Review the confirmation scope and keep a verified backup first. Deleting the
record does not remove files from Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin.

Season rows can also offer **Edit season date**, **Mark season watched**, **Resync season**,
and season-level unwatched actions. Individual episode rows provide their own watched,
unwatched, and watch-date actions. If an identity is repaired, corrected TV metadata and posters
are warmed before the refreshed show details return.

## TV show detail sections


When provider data is available, the page can render:

### Cast

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-cast-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-cast-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt TV show detail page showing credited cast members"
  caption="Cast cards open person pages with biographies and filmographies."
/>

The **Cast** section lists credited people and their roles. Select a person to inspect their
biography without changing the show record.

### Images

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-images-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-images-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt TV show detail page showing the Images gallery"
  caption="Images is a provider gallery separate from the saved poster, logo, and background artwork."
/>

The **Images** gallery opens scene artwork in a lightbox. It is separate from the artwork
choices under Edit Images.

### Trailers & Clips

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-trailers-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-trailers-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Lioness TV show detail page showing four populated Trailers and Clips video cards"
  caption="Trailers & Clips shows provider video previews with titles and play controls on the selected show."
/>

Select a trailer or clip to play it in the media lightbox. A missing video is a provider-data
condition, not a watched-state change.

### Reviews

The **Reviews** section displays provider reviews and scores when that provider is enabled.
Review content is provider-supplied and remains separate from your private [Ratings](/docs/ratings/).

### Related Shows

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-related-focus-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/tv-related-focus-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Lioness TV show detail page showing a populated Related Shows rail"
  caption="Related Shows keeps provider recommendations visible as populated poster cards."
/>

The **Related Shows** section links to provider-related series. It does not add those shows
to the local library automatically.

These sections belong to the selected series. They are not additional library pages.

## Appearance

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-appearance-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-appearance-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Appearance menu on a TV show detail page showing optional section switches"
  caption="The full Appearance panel shows the switches that control optional TV detail sections."
/>

Open [Appearance](/docs/appearance/#media-detail-options) in the sidebar while this TV show media page is active. The
TV-specific switches control which optional sections are rendered:

- **Logo Art**
- **Cast Members**
- **Trailers & Clips**
- **Reviews**
- **Images**
- **Related Shows**

Each switch changes presentation only and is saved as an appearance preference. The
Dashboard's history remains Card View by default; its layout is not controlled here.

For the shared behavior of watch dates, matching, artwork, and sync, see
[Media details and actions](/docs/media-details/).
