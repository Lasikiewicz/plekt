Discover is Plekt's external-media browsing page. It brings current TMDB feeds into the
same poster-card language as the local libraries, so you can explore without first adding a
title to a media server.


## Discovery rails


The default **Movies & TV** view can show:

- **Recommended for you**, based on movies and TV shows watched in the last 12 months;
- **Trending movies**;
- **Trending TV shows**;
- **In theatres** movies.

The exact titles change as TMDB updates its feeds. The page reports the number of results in
each rail and uses horizontal scrolling for the poster-card rows.

Discover is a feed, not a second local library. Recommended titles are built from distinct
titles watched in the last 12 months and cached per watched title. Titles already in your watch
history are removed from every rail, including recommendations. A title can appear here before
it exists on one of your connected media servers.

On a recommendation card, open **More options** and choose **Don't recommend** to remove that
movie or show from the personalized rail. The choice is saved by Plekt and continues to apply
after refreshes and on other browsers. It affects personalized recommendations only; trending,
popular, and current-release rails are unchanged.

## Type and genre filters


Use **Type** to switch between:

- **Movies & TV** for the combined feed;
- **Movies** for movie rails and movie genres; and
- **TV shows** for TV rails and TV genres.

The **Genre** selector changes with the chosen type. Select **All genres** to return to the
default feeds, or choose a genre to request filtered movie or TV rails. **Refresh** forces a
new feed request when you want to recheck the current snapshot.

Discover uses a short-lived local cache so a previously loaded feed can paint quickly while
the server revalidates it. A current TMDB API key is required. If the key is missing or
TMDB is temporarily unavailable, the page presents an actionable [Settings → Metadata](/docs/settings/metadata/) link
or a retry button instead of silently showing stale empty rails.

If there is not enough watch history to build recommendations, the page explains that the
personalized rail will appear after you watch a few titles. The other discovery rails remain
available.

## Open and save a title

Select a card poster or title to open the standard movie or TV detail page. Cards also keep
the common **More options** action available.

From a Discover card or detail page you can:

- add a movie or show to **Watchlist**;
- choose a personal **Rating** from 1 to 10;
- add the title to a **Playlist**; and
- choose **Don't recommend** to exclude it from the personalized recommendation rail.

Those actions are private local choices. They do not create a watched event, alter the
canonical provider match, or imply that a title is available in one of your libraries.
Use the detail page's **Available on** and **Watch Now** information to decide where a title
can actually be played.

For the pages that hold these choices, use the dedicated [Watchlist](/docs/watchlist/),
[Ratings](/docs/ratings/), and [Playlists](/docs/playlists/) pages for the private
workflows.
