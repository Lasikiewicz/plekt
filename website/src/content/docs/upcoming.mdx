Upcoming is a calendar of historical and future episode air dates for tracked shows.
Air dates are metadata: viewing an entry does not mark an episode watched.

Use [Up Next](/docs/dashboard/#up-next) on the Dashboard when you want a short list of
released, unwatched episodes that are ready to start. Upcoming remains the broader calendar
view.


## Use the calendar


1. Open **Upcoming**.
2. Use **Previous month**, **Next month**, or **Today** to move through the calendar.
3. Scroll to load adjacent months.
4. Enter a show, episode title, or episode code such as `S2E4` in the labelled **Search** field.
   The control bar buttons use the same pill style as the media page actions.
5. Select an episode when you need to open its show context.

The calendar uses TheTVDB-backed season and episode structure. Cached months open quickly,
while revalidation can refresh changed air dates in the background.

## Subscribe in a calendar app

Select **Subscribe** to create a private calendar URL for TV releases, movies, or both.
The feed includes upcoming releases for titles in your library. It uses the public address
you opened in your browser, unless `PLEKT_PUBLIC_URL` is configured. The address must be
reachable by your calendar provider. Local and private addresses cannot be used.

Anyone with the URL can read the feed. Regenerate it to revoke the previous URL, including
when changing which types of release are included.

## What Upcoming does not change

Upcoming does not create a watch event, change watched state, or update resume progress.
Use the episode or show detail actions when you want to make a watch-state correction.
