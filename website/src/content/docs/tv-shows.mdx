TV Shows groups tracked episode records into series and adds season, progress, and
next-airing context.


## Find a show

Use **Dropped only** to find shows you have removed from Up Next without deleting their
watch history or resume progress.


1. Open **TV Shows** in the sidebar.
2. Use the **Posters only** switch at the front of the control bar to fold every show to its
   poster; select a folded poster to open its card in place.
3. Search by show title or use the sort and A–Z controls.
4. Switch between the card grid, list view, and overview cards.
5. Use **Hide Fully Watched** to remove shows with no remaining unwatched episodes.
6. Use **Hide Ended** to remove ended shows when focusing on continuing series.
7. Select a full show card to open its TV show media page.

The **Posters only** choice is remembered for TV Shows separately from Movies. When it is
on, select a folded poster to open that show card in place; the other cards stay where they
are. A further selection opens the TV show media page. See the
[Shared library controls](/docs/libraries/#shared-library-controls).

The Posters only switch and both hide toggles sit directly in the control bar; there is no
separate Options menu.

Large libraries load progressively as you scroll. The shows already on screen stay in place
while more load, and while the list re-sorts by **Next Airing**. Saved pages are refreshed
after 30 minutes even when nothing was watched, so next-airing dates stay current. On a show
media page, season episode details load when a season is expanded so the initial summary is
not held up by every season's episode payload.

## Show cards and progress

  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/tvshows-dark.png"

  alt="Plekt TV Shows library cards showing each show's summary, last played date, and apps used"
  caption="Each TV library card represents a show, with its summary, last played date, and the apps used."
/>

A TV library card represents the show. It can include the show's summary,
watched-versus-total progress, last-play information with the **App Used** for the latest
watch, and the next airing date. When a show has several matching entries, Plekt groups them
under one card and picks its poster from those entries; choose a different poster in
**Edit Images** if the one shown is wrong. The list view can expand a show into seasons and episodes,
but the selected show's media page is where title-level actions live.

Unaired episodes are not treated as completed watches by a bulk action. If the title,
episode count, or progress is wrong, correct the series identity with
[Fix Match](/docs/media-details/#fix-match) before changing a whole season.

The child [TV Shows media page](/docs/tv-shows/media-page/) guide is about one selected
series, including its header, seasons, episodes, progress, actions, detail sections, and
page-specific Appearance controls.
