Manual Watch review is the decision queue for watch events that do not have a reliable
completion time. It keeps Plekt from assigning an invented date and gives you a focused
place to confirm, correct, or dismiss the event.

## How the review queue works

Enable **Require review** in [Settings → Sync → Sync tuning](/docs/settings/sync/#sync-tuning).
When Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin reports a provider-side watched flag without enough timing
information, Plekt keeps the event pending and shows **Manual Watch review** in the
sidebar. This includes an item that Plekt currently considers unwatched: the provider
event is held for a decision instead of being silently rejected or sent on to the other
media servers. The link is shown while there are decisions to make, including when a new
item is flagged after you already decided an earlier review.

Episode reviews are grouped by show, while movies appear as individual items. Use the search
field to narrow the queue, then open a show group when you want to process its episodes together.

## Choose a watch date

For a movie or a single episode, choose the date policy that matches what happened:

- **Mark watched now** records the current date and time.
- **Use release day** uses the movie or episode release date.
- **Use episode timing** uses nearby episode dates when that context is available.
- **Choose date & time** records a specific custom date.

Each season and show group has one watched and one unwatched button, such as **Mark all
reviewed episodes watched** and **Mark all reviewed episodes unwatched**. The watched button
opens the same date popup as the [media page's season and show actions](/docs/tv-shows/media-page/#watch-state-actions),
and the unwatched button uses the same unwatch confirmation. Only the episodes waiting for
review change; other episodes of the season or show keep their state. Review the resulting date
before confirming it.

Each review names the show its provider ids prove, and shows no poster rather than the wrong
show's. A review without provider ids is looked up through the reporting app and stays pending
instead of guessing when two shows share the title. The sidebar count matches the page.

## Correct provider state

Choose **Confirm** to record the watch in Plekt and send the normal watched-state
correction to the connected providers. Until that decision, Plekt does not propagate the
provider's watched flag. Choose **Dismiss & mark unwatched** when the provider event was
wrong; Plekt clears the local review and corrects the provider state before closing the
item.

Use the show-level buttons when every pending episode in a group should receive the same
decision. If a provider correction fails, the review remains pending so it can be retried. An
explicit unwatch clears an existing review, while a later deliberate provider event can create
a new one.

## Recheck status

Use **Recheck status** when a watch was already handled in another client or the queue looks
stale. Plekt rereads its canonical watch state and the pending review queue, then removes
items that are already marked watched in Plekt. This keeps provider leftovers from appearing
as decisions that still need action. Library imports also skip items that are already watched,
and pending reviews that local state already answers are closed automatically.

For delivery outcomes and targeted retries, see [Sync Activity](/docs/sync-tools/). For the
policy and timing controls that feed this queue, see [Settings → Sync](/docs/settings/sync/).
