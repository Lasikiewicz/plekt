Drop is for a show or movie you have stopped watching. A dropped title leaves
[Up Next](/docs/up-next/) and is left out of what Plekt pushes to your media apps, while its watch
dates, play counts, and resume progress stay exactly as they were.

## Works on

| App | Works | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plex | Yes | The title is hidden from Continue Watching. |
| Emby | Yes | The title is hidden from Continue Watching (Resume). |
| Jellyfin | Partly | Plekt stops listing the title, but an entry already on Next Up can stay visible. |
| Trakt | Shows only | Drop syncs both ways for shows. Movies stay local. |
| Simkl | Yes, with limits | Drop moves the title to Simkl's Dropped list and Un-drop restores its earlier list. A movie in Watching counts as watched, so Un-drop always returns movies to Plan to Watch. A title that was Completed before the drop is held rather than moved back. Simkl changes wait for the next watch or media-server event. |

**Why it differs.** Plex and Emby each have a separate hide for their rail. Jellyfin has no hide
that keeps resume progress, so Plekt cannot remove what is already on Next Up. Plekt syncs a drop to
Trakt for shows only, and Simkl's list rules limit what Un-drop can safely put back.

## Drop a title

- On an Up Next card, open **More options** and choose **Drop**.
- On a movie or show page, use **Drop** in the page actions.

Plekt saves the drop first. It then asks Plex and Emby to hide the matching rail entry. If an app
refuses, the drop still stands and Plekt reports the failure instead of reversing it.

## Bring a title back

Choose **Un-drop** from the same menus, or from the **Dropped only** view below. Un-drop makes the
title eligible for Up Next again but does not force an app's rail entry back into view; the next
normal playback or app refresh does that.

A completed live play dated after the drop also restores the title on its own. Played flags,
imports, history backfills, and tracker pulls do not.

## Find dropped titles

- **History**, **Movies**, and **TV Shows** each have a **Dropped only** control. It lists dropped
  titles, including ones with no watch history, and follows the page's search and media type.
- [Stats](/docs/stats/) shows a separate **Dropped** count, labelled all time regardless of the
  period you pick.

## Drop or remove?

**Remove from Up Next** is a "not now" choice that can undo itself when newer playback arrives, and
it is tracked in the Dismissed Up Next list. **Drop** is for a title you are done with. See
[Remove and restore](/docs/up-next/#remove-and-restore).
