Up Next is the [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard/)'s continuation rail. It combines actionable resume
records for movies and episodes with released **next up** episodes from tracked shows. Resume
progress lives here; it is not a separate history rail.

## Works on

| App | Rail Plekt updates | Removing a card | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plex | Continue Watching | Plekt hides the matching item. | Plex lists the next episode with no position, so it appears as a next-up card. |
| Emby | Continue Watching (Resume) | Plekt hides the matching item. | |
| Jellyfin | Next Up | Not possible. | Next Up holds one episode per show and no movies. Plekt keeps its own queue authoritative while Jellyfin catches up. |
| Trakt, Simkl | Not applicable | Not applicable | They are not part of the queue. |

**Why it differs.** These rails are calculated by each app, so Plekt cannot hand an app an
arbitrary item. For a ready episode it restamps the episode watched just before it, and the app
works out the rest. Jellyfin's Next Up is a read-only feed with no per-item removal, which is why a
removed or dropped title can linger there. In every app the push only adds, so it never hides
something that is missing from Plekt's queue.

## How the queue is built

- Resume cards are ordered by their latest progress update. Next-up cards follow in show, season,
  and episode order.
- Each show appears once. A part-watched episode with a known position replaces that show's
  next-up card, and of several part-watched episodes the most recently updated one is kept.
- The episode or movie playing right now moves to the top while it plays.
- Shows that share a title but have different TMDB or TVDB ids stay separate cards, and a
  re-release or reboot from a different year is not merged.
- When your latest play is behind the furthest episode you have watched, the episode after it gets
  its own card beside the show's ordinary next episode. See [Rewatch tracking](/docs/rewatch-tracking/).

Each card shows the episode name, its season and episode code, and **Watch Now** links that open
a matching item in Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin when a connected server can provide one. A cached local
resume card stays useful without a provider connection.

## Card actions

Use a card's **More options** menu for **Mark Watched**, **Rate**, **Add to watch list**,
**Clear Progress** (when resume exists), **Remove from Up Next**, and **Drop**. Mark Watched
commits locally first and then goes out through the normal sync path.

## What each app receives

When **Sync Up Next to media apps** is on in
[Settings → Sync → Sync tuning](/docs/settings/sync/#sync-tuning) (it is by default), Plekt queues
one background push whenever the queue changes, even with the Dashboard closed. Use **Sync Up
Next** for an immediate push.

The [Works on](#works-on) table shows the rail each app uses. For a ready episode, Plekt checks that it is unwatched, released, and follows a fully watched run.
It then refreshes the app's calculated rail from the watched episode just before it. Genuine
part-watches in an app are never overwritten.

The push only adds. An item that an app lists but Plekt's queue does not is left in place and
reported as an app-only item. An item leaves an app's rail only when you clear its progress or
remove it in Plekt, so stale entries you never touched can stay on a rail until the app drops them
or you remove them. To take a whole title out of the queue everywhere, use
[Drop](/docs/drop-titles/).

If an app does not answer, Plekt skips it, keeps its last good observation, and queues a new push
as soon as it answers again. Turning the setting off leaves those app lists unchanged while Plekt's
own Up Next stays available.

## Remove and restore

**Remove from Up Next** hides the card at once, clears its local resume progress, marks it
unwatched, and records the removal on the server, so it applies on every device. When a show has
two cards because of a rewatch, Plekt asks whether to hide only that card's season or the whole show.

When items are removed, the count button beside the **Up Next** heading opens **Dismissed Up
Next**. Choose **Add back** for one item or **Add all back** to restore cards. Plekt then pushes the
restored queue to the connected apps. A removal is a "not now" choice, not a permanent delete: the
item can return after newer genuine playback progress is recorded.
