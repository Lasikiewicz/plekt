Watchlist is the private “save this for later” page. It contains movies and TV shows that
you want to revisit. Episode ratings are supported separately on [Ratings](/docs/ratings/),
but episodes are not Watchlist entries. Watchlist is not a media-server queue and it never
marks a title watched by itself.


## Open Watchlist

Select **Watchlist** in the sidebar. Cards load from Plekt's local private-data store,
so the page remains useful even when a provider feed or media server is temporarily
unavailable.

## Saved media cards

<ScreenshotFigure

Cards represent a movie or a TV show. Select the poster or title to open the normal detail
page. The card can also expose the current personal rating and list membership without
changing the underlying watch record.

## Add a title

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-light.png"
  alt="Plekt media detail page showing Add to watch list beside the other title actions"
  caption="Add to watch list is available on the detail action row when the title is not saved."
/>

Use **Add to watch list** from a movie or TV detail page, or use the corresponding card
action from Discover or another supported media card. When the request succeeds, the action
changes to **Remove from watch list** and the title appears on this page. Episode detail
pages expose personal rating actions, not Watchlist actions.

Adding a title does not mark it watched, add it to a connected server, or change its TMDB,
TVDB, Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin identity.

## Sync with Plex

Watchlist sync is optional and is controlled by one **Sync watchlist with Plex** toggle in
Settings. Plex is the only watchlist provider: its account-level Universal Watchlist can
hold titles that are not already in a local library, while Emby and Jellyfin do not provide
the same watchlist contract.

The first sync uses a safe union of the local and Plex lists. Later complete Plex snapshots
reconcile additions and removals; an outage or incomplete snapshot cannot delete the local
canonical list. A Plex-only addition is imported locally, and local changes are delivered
through a durable queue. A common or duplicate title is resolved by year and then by the
item's provider ids, so two separate entries such as two *Arrival* (2016) titles each find the
right Plex item.

Use **Sync now** for a full reconcile. Failed, unavailable, or reauthentication-required
changes stay visible in the Sync panel with a **Retry queued changes** action. Reconnecting
Plex or retrying does not roll back the local list.

## Rate or remove a card

Use **Rate** to open the 1–10 personal rating control. The score is also visible from the
[Ratings page](/docs/ratings/). Use **Remove** to take the item out of Watchlist; removing
it does not remove its rating, watch history, or custom-list membership.

The page updates in place after either action. If the card does not change, reopen
Watchlist and check the signed-in instance before trying the action again.

## More options and detail actions

Use **More options** when you need an action that is not shown as a primary card button.
Open the title detail page when you need watch dates, artwork, Fix Match, Force Sync, or the
full Tools menu. Personal actions and maintenance tools are intentionally separate.

## Empty state and privacy

An empty Watchlist means no titles are currently saved; it does not mean the library or
Discover feed is empty. Watchlist data belongs to the local Plekt instance and does not
become a watched event. If Plex sync is enabled, its provider changes appear as Watchlist
sync activity, but they are not watched-state destinations.

For the related pages, see [Ratings](/docs/ratings/) and [Playlists](/docs/playlists/).
