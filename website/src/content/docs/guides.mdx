Guides are task-focused paths through the current Plekt interface.

Use this page to choose where to start. The linked pages are the canonical references for
labels, controls, settings, and routes, so this index points to them instead of repeating
the same procedures in several places.

## Start and orient

- [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/) covers installation, claiming the instance, and the in-app Guided Setup flow.
- [Upgrading from Plembfin to Plekt](/docs/upgrading-from-plembfin/) preserves an existing instance through the rename, image update, and port choice.
- [Navigation and global controls](/docs/navigation/) explains the sidebar, status indicator, theme control, and shared page controls.
- [The sync model](/docs/concepts/) explains canonical watch state, resume progress, rewatches, and delivery evidence.

## Use the media pages

- [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard/) covers Now Playing, Up Next, resume progress, and watch history.
- [Movies](/docs/movies/) covers the movie library and its quick actions.
- [TV Shows](/docs/tv-shows/) covers show progress, seasons, episodes, and show actions.
- [Upcoming](/docs/upcoming/) covers the episode calendar.
- [Media details and actions](/docs/media-details/) covers watch dates, matching, artwork, Tools, and related content.
- [Discover](/docs/discover/) covers current TMDB-backed feeds and title actions.

## Keep personal lists

- [Watchlist](/docs/watchlist/) is the private save-for-later page.
- [Ratings](/docs/ratings/) stores private 1–10 scores for movies, shows, and episodes.
- [Playlists](/docs/playlists/) holds ordered collections that can sync to Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin.

## Connect and maintain

- [Integrations](/docs/integrations/) explains media-server, tracker, request, metadata, and artwork connections.
- [Settings](/docs/settings/) is the map for the instance configuration pages.
- [Sync Activity and tools](/docs/sync-tools/) is the evidence and recovery guide for delivery results, retries, and Force Sync.
- [Backups and operations](/docs/operations/) covers backups, restores, imports, security, and routine maintenance.
- [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) starts from a symptom and leads to the smallest safe correction.

## Keep the paths clear

Choose the page that owns the task:

- use a media-page guide for title-specific actions and detail sections;
- use a dedicated Watchlist, Ratings, or Playlists guide for personal media;
- use the relevant Settings page for configuration values and maintenance tools; and
- use Troubleshooting when the expected result did not appear.

This keeps the guide section useful without maintaining parallel copies of the same
instructions.
