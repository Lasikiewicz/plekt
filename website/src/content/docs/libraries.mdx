The [Movies](/docs/movies/) and [TV Shows](/docs/tv-shows/) pages are tracked libraries built
from Plekt's local watch records. They help you find a title that Plekt knows about;
they are not raw file browsers for every connected server.

## Shared library controls

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/movies-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/movies-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Movies library showing search, sort, poster size, and view controls"
  caption="Shared library controls change how the loaded records are searched, ordered, and presented."
/>

Both libraries provide a similar set of browsing controls.

The exact available sort values depend on the page and the loaded data.

- **Size** leads the control bar and changes how many poster cards fit across the page.
- **Search** narrows the loaded titles by the title and available library fields.
- **Sort** changes the order, including title, watch date, and the available next-airing order.
- **Poster, list, or overview-card view** changes how much information is visible while browsing.
- **Posters only** folds cards to their posters and is remembered separately for Movies and
  TV Shows.
- **A–Z** jumps to a letter in the loaded result set.
- **Dropped only** limits the Movies or TV Shows library to titles marked dropped.

Every control sits directly in the control bar; there is no separate Options menu.
- Selecting a poster opens the relevant [media detail page](/docs/media-details/).

With **Posters only** on, select a folded poster to open its card in place. Other cards stay
where they are, and selecting the open card again opens its media detail page. Library cards
keep the **Watched Twice** or **Watched N Times** label when a title has multiple recorded
watches; Dashboard history cards omit that line.

Large libraries load progressively as you scroll.

A card's state or availability indicator describes the latest known record and can change
after a sync or metadata refresh.

## The shared card controls

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/library-poster-menu-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/library-poster-menu-light.png"
  alt="Plekt movie poster card with the shared three-dot quick-actions menu open"
  caption="The poster three-dot menu exposes quick actions without leaving the library."
/>

The three-dot menu is the quick-action menu on movie, TV, and supported History cards.

On a desktop pointer, hover the poster card to reveal the button.

Open it and choose:

- **Edit watch date** to inspect, change, add, or remove a recorded viewing date;
- **Fix match** to correct the provider identity attached to the local record;
- **Rate** to set a private local score;
- **Add to watch list** to save a title for later;
- **Add to playlist** to pick a [playlist](/docs/playlists/) or create one; and
- **Mark unwatched** to clear the current watched state and propagate that correction.

The card refreshes in place after the action.

The menu does not replace the detail-page tools. Use the relevant [movie](/docs/movies/)
or [TV Shows](/docs/tv-shows/) guide for library-specific behavior, and
[Media details and actions](/docs/media-details/) for artwork, merge, delete, title-level
Force Sync, trailers, galleries, and the full watch history.

If the menu is missing, move the pointer over the card and check that the card is not
covered by another element.

Open the title detail page as a fallback.

Follow [the three-dot menu troubleshooting guide](/docs/troubleshooting/#the-poster-three-dot-menu-is-missing-or-does-nothing)
if the button still does nothing.

For title-specific behavior, use [Movies](/docs/movies/), [TV Shows](/docs/tv-shows/), and
[Media details and actions](/docs/media-details/). This page only documents controls shared
by both libraries.
