Playlists are ordered collections of movies, TV episodes, or both. You pick the titles
yourself, or let an [automatic playlist](#automatic-playlists) fill itself from a rule. A
playlist can stay in Plekt only, or sync to any connected Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin server.
Playlists never change watched state.

## Open Playlists

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/playlists-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/playlists-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Playlists page showing playlist rails with app badges, item counts, and Options menus"
  caption="Playlist rails show their type, synced apps, item count, and ordered titles."
/>

Select **Playlists** in the sidebar, just above Ratings. Each playlist is its own rail. The
first four show together; more playlists follow below. Old `/custom-lists` links open this
page.

Each heading shows:

- the playlist type: **Movies**, **TV**, or **Mixed**;
- an app badge for each app it syncs to. Hover a badge for its status: synced, waiting for
  the first sync, failed, or not found. **Plekt only** means no app is selected;
- the item count, and an **Auto** tag on automatic playlists;
- an **Options** menu with **Edit** and **Delete**.

The page updates itself when a scheduled sync changes a playlist.

When an app holds items Plekt could not identify, a note under the heading says how many.
Those items stay in that app untouched.

## Create a playlist

1. Choose **New playlist**, then **Pick items** (or **Automatic**, see below).
2. On **Name and type**, enter a name and choose **Movies**, **TV**, or **Mixed**. The type
   cannot change later.
3. Leave **Remove items once watched** on to drop titles from the playlist once you watch
   them, or turn it off to keep them.
4. On **Apps**, tick the apps to sync to. Only connected apps can be ticked; see
   [Settings → Media servers](/docs/settings/media-servers/). Leave every app unticked to
   keep the playlist in Plekt only.

## Automatic playlists

An automatic playlist fills itself from a rule. Choose **New playlist → Automatic**, then set
the rule:

- **Type**: Top rated (the default), Popular, Trending this week, or New releases.
- **Source**: your libraries, or the whole TMDB catalogue.
- Genres, original language (English is ticked on a new playlist), years or a decade, watched
  state (movies only), and recently added (library source only).
- **Maximum** titles (20 by default) and the order: ranked, or by full release date.

Talk and news shows, and library titles not yet matched to TMDB, IMDb, or TVDB, are left out.
An automatic playlist can be Movies or TV, not Mixed. A TV one holds the next episode to watch
of each show.

Plekt checks the rule every hour. A new playlist shows **Checking the rule** until its
titles arrive. The **Options** menu adds **Info** (the full rule), **Refresh now**, and
**Stop updating**.

Automatic playlists are sent one way, from Plekt to the apps. With **Remove items once
watched** on, watched titles leave the playlist. With it off, a watched TV show moves on to
its next episode at the next sync.

## Import from apps

Choose **Import from apps** beside **New playlist** to bring in playlists made in Plex, Emby,
or Jellyfin. The dialog lists each app's playlists with their item count and a type guess.
When a name matches an existing Plekt playlist, choose **Merge** to add to it or **Import
separately** to keep a second playlist.

## How app sync works

- Each app's copy holds only titles in that app's library. A title the app lacks stays in
  the Plekt playlist with a **Missing from** note (a **Missing** badge on posters) and
  joins the app's copy once it arrives.
- TV and Mixed playlists hold individual episodes, not whole shows.
- The app playlists follow Plekt's order, and moves made in an app are read back.
- A Plekt edit syncs a few seconds later. A background pass also runs every 5 minutes.

### Changes made in an app

Plekt also picks up changes made to the playlist inside an app:

- A title added in the app is added in Plekt and to the other selected apps.
- A title removed in the app is removed in Plekt, but only while the app's library still
  has it. A title that left the library stays, marked **Missing from** that app.
- Removing one episode of a synced show keeps that episode out on later syncs.
- A playlist deleted in the app is deleted everywhere and moves to
  [Recently deleted](#recently-deleted).

A failed or empty read from an app never counts as a removal. When one sync would remove
many titles at once, or an app reports several playlists deleted at once, Plekt
holds the change and shows a banner above the rail. Choose **Confirm** to apply it or
**Discard** to restore the app's copy.

## Add a movie or TV show

  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-light.png"
  alt="Plekt media detail action row with the playlist action"
  caption="The playlist action on a movie or TV detail page opens the playlist chooser."
/>

Use **Add to playlist** on a movie or TV detail page, or the **Add to playlist** submenu in
a poster card's menu. Pick a playlist, or choose **Create a new playlist**. Only playlists
that accept the title are offered, and automatic playlists are left out.

Adding a show to a TV or Mixed playlist opens the episode picker. Tick episodes one by one or
a whole season; episodes already in the playlist show ticked. New titles join the top of the
playlist.

A title can be in several playlists and also in [Watchlist](/docs/watchlist/) or
[Ratings](/docs/ratings/). With **Posters only** on, neighbouring episodes of one show stack
into a single poster.

## Reorder and remove titles

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/playlists-cards-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/playlists-cards-light.png"
  alt="Plekt playlist cards with the Move pill"
  caption="Drag a card to reorder it on desktop, or use its Move pill on a touch screen."
/>

- On desktop, drag a card within its rail. The other cards slide aside to show where it will
  land.
- On a touch screen, use the **Move** pill at the bottom of a full card.
- **Remove from this playlist** in the card menu removes the title from this playlist only.
  Other playlists, Watchlist, Ratings, the library, and watch history stay unchanged.

Automatic playlists follow their rule, so their cards cannot be dragged or moved.

## Edit or delete a playlist

- **Edit** renames the playlist or changes its apps. Unticking an app deletes the playlist
  from that app. An automatic playlist can also change its rule.
- **Delete** asks first, then moves the playlist to Recently deleted and deletes it from its
  synced apps.

## Recently deleted

The **Recently deleted** button (with a count) appears while a deleted playlist is waiting.
It opens a list showing who deleted each playlist (Plekt or an app) and when.

- **Restore** brings the playlist back in Plekt and in every app it synced to.
- **Delete permanently** removes it for good. It cannot be undone.
- **Delete all permanently** clears the whole list after one confirmation.

## Privacy and watched state

Playlists are local choices. They never create watched events, change ratings, or change the
sync identity of a title. For the other private workflows, see [Watchlist](/docs/watchlist/)
and [Ratings](/docs/ratings/).
