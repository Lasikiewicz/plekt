Ratings is the private scorebook for your Plekt instance. It supports a 1–10 score for
movies, TV shows, and episodes and keeps that score separate from provider ratings and
canonical watched state.

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/ratings-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/ratings-light.png"
  alt="Plekt Ratings overview showing personal movie, show, and episode ratings"
  caption="Ratings groups your personal scores by media type while keeping provider ratings separate."
/>

## Works on

| App | Works | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plex | Yes | Two-way. Off by default. |
| Emby | Yes | Two-way. Off by default. |
| Jellyfin | Yes | Two-way. Off by default. Clearing a rating sends a rating of 0, because Jellyfin ignores an empty value. |
| Trakt | Yes | Two-way. Off by default. |
| Simkl | Partly | Movies and shows only, no anime or episodes. A write needs a movie already completed with a known watch date, or a show already in a Simkl list. Unsafe writes are held. A missing Simkl rating never clears yours; only an explicit empty rating does. |

**Why it differs.** Each service stores ratings on its own terms. Simkl only accepts a rating on a
title it already tracks as watched or listed, so Plekt holds writes that would fail or create a
false watch.

Select **Ratings** in the sidebar. The page groups existing scores under **Movies**, **TV
Shows**, and **Episodes**, so a show-level score is not confused with one of its episodes.

## Rate a title from a card

Choose **Rate** on an unrated card. On a rated card, select its current score (for example
8/10) to change it. Select a value from 1 to 10 and save it. The updated score is written to Plekt's local private data and the
card remains in its media-type group.

## Rate from a detail page

On a movie or TV detail page, use the personal rating control in the media facts area. It
shows the current value when one exists and opens the same 1–10 chooser when you want to
change it. Episode detail and episode-card actions use the same personal rating domain. This
control is distinct from TMDB, IMDb, or TheTVDB provider ratings.

## Change or remove a rating

Use the rating control on the card or detail page to replace a score. Use **Remove rating** to
clear the personal score.
Neither action removes the title from Watchlist or Playlists, changes watch history, or
sends a watched-state correction to a connected service.

## Personal Rating Sync

Personal Rating Sync can include Simkl as well as supported media servers and Trakt.

Rating sync is optional and disabled by default. Enable the single **Sync personal ratings**
toggle under [Settings → Sync → Personal Rating Sync](/docs/settings/sync/#personal-rating-sync) to reconcile ratings with every
connected Plex, Emby, Jellyfin, and Trakt account. Plekt remains the conflict authority. A
rating added, changed, or cleared directly on one service is imported and sent out to every
other service, unless a local edit is genuinely newer. An unchanged rating is not re-imported
as a remote change on later scans.

The first complete provider snapshot imports existing ratings. Later complete snapshots
reconcile additions, edits, and removals; a partial or failed read cannot clear the local
scorebook. Local changes commit to Plekt first and are delivered through a durable queue,
so a provider outage does not roll back a rating or affect watched state.

If a later scan finds at least two previously rated items and more than 20% of that
provider's ratings missing, Plekt holds the removals for review instead of clearing them.
The panel shows **Rating scan held for review** with the provider, counts and missing titles.
**Approve these removals** rechecks the provider and applies the same removals only if they
still match; **Dismiss and keep ratings** keeps every rating. Scheduled scans cannot bypass a
hold, and holds survive restarts.

Use **Sync now** for an immediate full pass. The panel reports provider status, queued work,
unmatched items, reauthentication requirements, and retryable failures. Episode ratings keep
their parent-show identity plus season and episode coordinates, so the same episode does not
become multiple ratings when provider IDs differ.

## Episode identity and groups

Episode scores retain the show, season, and episode coordinates. Separate provider records
for the same episode therefore resolve to one personal rating rather than creating a second
score for the same viewing unit.

## Relationship to watched state

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-light.png"
  alt="Plekt media detail page showing separate rating and watch-status controls"
  caption="Personal rating and Watch Status are separate controls on the detail page."
/>

A rating is personal metadata. It does not mark a title watched, create a watch event, add
resume progress, alter the provider match, or sync a watch-state change through Plex, Emby,
Jellyfin, or Trakt.

For saving and grouping titles, see [Watchlist](/docs/watchlist/) and
[Playlists](/docs/playlists/).
