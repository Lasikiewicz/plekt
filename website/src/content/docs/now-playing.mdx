Now Playing is session information, not completed watch history. It sits at the top of the
[Dashboard](/docs/dashboard/) and shows what is playing right now on your connected servers.

## Works on

| App | Works | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plex | Yes | Sessions arrive through Plex's notification connection and a session check. A user added to a Plex server that another Plekt user owns appears through the owner's connected account. |
| Emby | Yes, after setup | Needs the Plekt plugin or a webhook. See [Emby](/docs/emby/). |
| Jellyfin | Yes, after setup | Needs the Plekt plugin or a webhook. See [Jellyfin](/docs/jellyfin/). |
| Trakt, Simkl | Not applicable | They record history, not live playback. |

**Why it differs.** Each media server reports playback in its own way. Plex's own webhooks only
fire when playback changes state and send no "still playing" signal, so Plekt also checks Plex's
sessions on a schedule. Emby and Jellyfin do not tell an outside app about playback until a plugin
or webhook is set up. Trackers never host the player, so they have no sessions to show.

## What a session shows

A session card shows the title, the episode title with its season and episode, the source
service, the current position and duration, and a progress bar. Select the title to open its
[media details](/docs/media-details/).

## How fresh it is

- Playback checks run every 10 seconds while something is playing and every 5 minutes while idle.
- Opening the Dashboard or returning to a background tab requests a fresh snapshot at once.
- A plugin, webhook, or Plex notification reports a new session sooner than the idle check.
- **Active Session TTL** in [Settings → Sync → Sync tuning](/docs/settings/sync/#sync-tuning)
  controls when an inactive session is treated as stale.

Each user sees their own playback. A provider failure is kept separate from a genuinely empty
result, so a temporary connection problem does not claim that playback stopped. Disappearing from
Now Playing does not, by itself, create a completed watch.

## Panel options

The cog beside the panel heading opens its options. Every change saves at once and applies to the
whole server.

- **Allow part watched items here** and **Allow Up Next items here** are both on by default. Part
  watched items fill the free slots first, then the other [Up Next](/docs/up-next/) items.
- The item count (1 to 3) sets how many cards the panel holds, live sessions included.

While an episode or movie plays, its own Up Next card is hidden and never featured in the panel,
so the title shows once, as its live session.

If a session never appears, follow
[Now Playing is empty or stale](/docs/troubleshooting/#now-playing-is-empty-or-stale).
